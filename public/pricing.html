Questions

Before you apply.

What counts as an audit?

One full audit pass on a single solicitation — document classification, CLIN and FAR/DFARS review, and trap detection, verified against source text. Re-running an amended solicitation counts as a new audit.

How does access work?

You apply, we review your pipeline within one business day, and we onboard a limited number of design partners. Exact terms are shared during the review.

We bid mostly civilian agencies — does FARaudit cover that?

FARaudit is defense-first by design, and the same FAR engine reads civilian federal solicitations. If your pipeline is federal, it's in scope.
