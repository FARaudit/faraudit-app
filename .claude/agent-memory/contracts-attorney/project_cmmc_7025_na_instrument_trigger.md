Ruling (2026-07-21, N0016726Q1089 / audit 03aa1fbe): DFARS 252.204-7025 sub-obligations ((b)(2), (c), (d)) are conditional on the (b)(1) level fill-in; (b)(1)=N/A renders them dormant (204.7504(a) prescribes 7021/7025 only when a specific CMMC level is required; N/A is not a prescribed fill-in — boilerplate over-inclusion, patent-not-latent, waived post-close under 4 CFR 21.2(a)(1)). The NHR driver was 7025(a) DEFINITIONS text — definitions impose no obligation, can never be a disqualifier (benign-recital class).

**Why:** Brain #632's "same credential family via shared clause-authority cite (7021/32 CFR 170)" anchor FALSE-NEGATIVES on the specimen itself (grounded N/A cites **7025**(b)(1); the ungrounded fragment carries NO cite) and FALSE-POSITIVES on cross-clause bleed (252.204-7012/800-171 is NOT conditioned on the CMMC level — a 32 CFR 170 cite-match would wrongly reconcile it; NOTE: 7012 was a DESIGN-level risk, not present in this specimen, and its current text was not verified live — pull at acquisition.gov before relying) plus multi-instance/amendment-superseded fill-ins (7025 appeared in both §C and §I of this very specimen — grounded). Cite-string anchoring = re-run of the token/substring-collision doctrine.

**How to apply:** When reviewing conditional-clause reconciliation designs, demand instrument-scoped trigger inheritance AT EMISSION (sub-findings of one clause occurrence inherit that occurrence's grounded fill-in state; per-instance, positive shape-allowlist for "no level required"; heterogeneous instances → no suppression) + a definitions-are-not-obligations floor. Reject decide-layer cross-finding cite matching.
