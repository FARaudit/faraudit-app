# PRIMARY-SOURCE EVIDENCE — Non-Manufacturer Rule (Brain card 215 Fork B, Rule-64 freeze gate)

Fetched 2026-07-02 to ratify the NMR caution string BEFORE freeze. **Finding: Brain's caution is substantively correct, but the "≤500 employees" and "SBA waiver" facts are NOT in the 52.219-33 clause text — they live in 13 CFR 121.406. Attribution corrected below.**

## FAR 52.219-33 (the contract CLAUSE) — acquisition.gov/far/52.219-33
The clause states the nonmanufacturer conditions at (c)(1) — it does NOT state a numeric employee ceiling or a waiver mechanism:
- "Provide an end item that a small business has manufactured, processed, or produced in the United States or its outlying areas"
- "Be primarily engaged in the retail or wholesale trade and normally sell the type of item being supplied"
- "Take ownership or possession of the item(s) with its personnel, equipment, or facilities in a manner consistent with industry practice"
- Kit assemblers: "at least 50 percent of the total cost of the components … manufactured … by small business concerns"
- **No employee threshold and no waiver provision appear in 52.219-33 itself.**

## 13 CFR 121.406(b)(1) (the SBA SIZE regulation) — ecfr.gov / law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/13/121.406
- Employee ceiling: a firm qualifies as a nonmanufacturer if it **"does not exceed 500 employees"** (150 for the IT-VAR exception to NAICS 541519). ← the 1,100→500 claim VERIFIED, sourced HERE (not in 52.219-33).
- Must "supply the end item of a small business manufacturer, processor or producer made in the United States, **or obtain a waiver of such requirement**."
- **Waivers exist** (the first clause-only fetch missed them): CLASS waiver — SBA determines no small business manufacturer/processor of the product or class is available; INDIVIDUAL waiver — CO determines no small business manufacturer can reasonably be expected to offer, SBA accepts (contract-specific, 1-year, expires at contract end).

## RATIFIED CAUTION STRING (path-aware, attribution-correct — supersedes Brain's draft wording)
> "If supplying another manufacturer's product (nonmanufacturer), verify Nonmanufacturer Rule compliance (FAR 52.219-33): the offeror must not exceed 500 employees (SBA nonmanufacturer size standard, 13 CFR 121.406(b)) and must supply the end item of a small U.S. manufacturer, unless SBA grants a class or individual waiver."

The 500-ceiling applies ONLY on the nonmanufacturer (reseller) path — it does NOT replace the solicitation's stated NAICS 337214 size standard (1,100 employees) for a manufacturer offeror. Both coexist; the caution is conditional ("if supplying another manufacturer's product"), never an assertion that the firm IS ineligible.
