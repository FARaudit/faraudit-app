SECTION A — REQUEST FOR QUOTATION
Issuing Office: General Services Administration (GSA)

SCOPE
Complete facilities maintenance services at seventeen (17) locations throughout the State of Georgia.

PRIOR-EXPERIENCE EVALUATION
A quotation will be viewed as having an unfavorable aspect if the prior-experience projects submitted are not inclusive of two or more geographically dispersed facilities. A distance of less than 200 miles between at least two of the locations performed under a single project will be viewed as an unfavorable aspect. Submitted experience must have been performed under one contract rather than under separate concurrent contracts.
