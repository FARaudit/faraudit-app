SECTION A — SOLICITATION
Solicitation Number: 1202SC24R2100
Issuing Office: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Forest Service, Incident Procurement

SET-ASIDE AND SIZE STANDARD
This acquisition is set aside one hundred percent (100%) for small business concerns under NAICS code 722310, Food Service Contractors, with a corresponding small business size standard of $47 million in average annual receipts. An offeror must qualify as small under the assigned NAICS code and size standard as of the date of its initial offer including price.

SCOPE
The Contractor shall provide Mobile Food Service Units (MFSU) and associated support for wildfire suppression and all-hazard incident response, including personnel, equipment, and mobile kitchen units.

FAR 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, applies. For a service contract, the concern will not pay more than 50% of the amount paid by the Government to it to firms that are not similarly situated; the concern must perform the primary and vital requirements with its own employees.
