SECTION A — REQUEST FOR QUOTATION
Solicitation Number: W912DY-25-Q-A101
Issuing Office: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Engineering and Support Center, Huntsville
Acquisition: Replacement appliances for multiple U.S. Navy installations

CLASSIFICATION
NAICS code: 335220, Major Household Appliance Manufacturing (size standard 1,500 employees). Product/Service Code: 7290. This is a full and open competition (no set-aside), a simplified acquisition estimated under $250,000.

SCOPE / DELIVERY LOCATIONS
The Contractor shall furnish and deliver replacement appliances, including microwaves, dehumidifiers, and refrigerator-freezers, for installation at multiple U.S. Navy installations, including Naval Station Great Lakes (IL), Naval Base Ventura County / Point Mugu (CA), Norfolk Naval Shipyard (VA), Naval Station Newport (RI), NAS Pensacola (FL), and NS Sasebo (Japan).

REQUIREMENTS
(a) Refrigerator-freezer units shall not exceed the critical dimensions of 22.5" W x 23" D x 57.25" H. Dimensions are critical.
(b) Refrigerators shall be Energy Star certified. (Per solicitation amendments, this requirement was waived and then reinstated.)
(c) All items shall comply with the Trade Agreements Act (TAA): items shall be U.S.-made, qualifying country, or designated country end products.

SUBMISSION
Quotes shall be submitted in accordance with the procedures herein no later than 12:00 PM Central Time, 28 August 2025. Required submittals: SF 1449, the pricing attachment, and the technical checklist.

EVALUATION
Best value determined by technical acceptability and lowest total price, considering past performance and schedule adherence.
