SECTION A — SOLICITATION
Solicitation Number: 36C25925Q0247
Issuing Office: Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office 19 (NCO19)

SET-ASIDE AND SIZE STANDARD
This acquisition is set aside one hundred percent (100%) for Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) concerns. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 238290, Other Building Equipment Contractors, with a corresponding small business size standard of $22 million in average annual receipts. To be eligible for award, an offeror must be a verified SDVOSB concern and must qualify as a small business under the assigned NAICS code and size standard as of the date of its offer.

SCOPE
The Contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, tools, parts, and materials necessary to perform preventive maintenance, inspection, testing, and repair of elevator systems at the identified VA facility.

FAR 52.219-14, Limitations on Subcontracting, applies to this set-aside acquisition. The concern must perform the applicable percentage of the work with its own employees and may not be unusually reliant on a subcontractor for the primary and vital requirements of the contract.
