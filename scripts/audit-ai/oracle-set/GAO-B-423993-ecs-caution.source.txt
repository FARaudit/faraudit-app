SECTION A — REQUEST FOR QUOTATION
Solicitation Number: W912DY-25-Q-A101
Issuing Office: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers

SCOPE / REQUIREMENTS
The Contractor shall furnish and deliver replacement refrigerators and household appliances for installation at the identified Government facility. Requirements include:
(a) Each unit shall conform to the critical dimensions specified herein, within a tolerance of one-half (1/2) inch.
(b) Refrigerators shall be Energy Star certified. (This requirement was removed by amendment and subsequently reinstated by a later amendment.)
(c) All items shall comply with the Trade Agreements Act (TAA).

REFERENCE PRODUCT
Suggested reference product: Vestel model 263 refrigerator (220-volt; country of manufacture: Turkey).

Offerors shall respond to Government technical-evaluation notices and solicitation amendments within the response windows stated in each notice or amendment.
