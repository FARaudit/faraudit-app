SECTION A — SOLICITATION
Solicitation Number: FA8903-24-R-0023
Issuing Office: United States Air Force

SET-ASIDE AND SIZE STANDARD
This solicitation establishes two pools: an unrestricted pool and a small business set-aside pool. The small business set-aside pool is set aside for small business concerns under NAICS code 236220, Commercial and Institutional Building Construction, with a corresponding small business size standard of $45 million in average annual receipts. To be eligible under the set-aside pool, an offeror must qualify as small under the assigned NAICS code and size standard as of the date it submits its offer, including price.

SCOPE
Comprehensive Construction and Engineering (C2E) for Facilities Sustainment, Restoration, and Modernization (FSRM) worldwide. Multiple awards are contemplated under each pool.
